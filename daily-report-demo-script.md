# Daily Report Consolidation — Video Demo Script

**Runtime:** ~75 seconds
**Setting:** Active residential build site, mid-afternoon
**Lead character:** MIKE — Site Superintendent, mid-40s, hi-vis vest, dusty boots, phone in hand
**Tone:** Practical, grounded. Feels like footage from a real jobsite, not a polished ad.

---

## SCENE 1 — MORNING CHAOS (0:00 – 0:12)

**VISUAL**
Wide shot of a half-framed two-story residential build. Three workers carrying lumber in the background. Mike stands near his pickup, tailgate down, papers flapping in the breeze. He's holding his phone in one hand and a clipboard in the other.

**ON-SCREEN ACTIONS**
- Quick cuts of Mike's phone screen:
  - WhatsApp message: *"Finished framing on Unit 3 — moving to sheathing"*
  - A second message: *"Concrete truck running 40 min late"*
  - A voice memo bubble (0:48 long)
  - A blurry photo of rebar
- Cut to clipboard: handwritten equipment hours, partially smudged.

**DIALOGUE (Mike, half to himself)**
> "Three WhatsApp groups, two voice memos, and I still don't know what the masons did this morning…"

**AUDIO**
- Distant nail gun: *tk-tk-tk*
- Light wind across the mic
- Faint chatter, a truck reversing beeper far off

**ON-SCREEN TEXT (small, lower-third)**
*"Daily report: usually 90 minutes at end of day"*

---

## SCENE 2 — AI CAPTURE IN REAL TIME (0:12 – 0:28)

**VISUAL**
Over-the-shoulder of Mike's phone. He opens an app called **"SiteLog"** (clean, simple — looks like a tool, not a toy). The Today tab is already filling itself in.

**ON-SCREEN ACTIONS**
- A crew member's voice note appears in the feed.
- Worker (off-screen, on the voice note): *"Hey Mike — Carlos and I wrapped up framing on Unit 3, about 6 hours each. Used the nail gun and the small compressor."*
- The voice note shrinks into a card that re-renders as a structured entry:

```
UNIT 3 — FRAMING
Crew: Carlos R., Diego M.
Hours: 6.0 each (12.0 total)
Equipment: Framing nailer, 2HP compressor
Status: Complete
```

**DIALOGUE (Mike, quietly impressed)**
> "Didn't have to type a thing."

**AUDIO**
- Drilling picks up briefly, then fades
- Soft notification chime from phone (single, not a jingle)

---

## SCENE 3 — PHOTO + CONTEXT (0:28 – 0:40)

**VISUAL**
Mike walks toward Unit 3. He raises his phone, snaps two quick photos — one of the completed framing, one of a small pile of damaged sheathing in the corner.

**ON-SCREEN ACTIONS**
The photos appear in the app with auto-generated tags fading in beside them:

**Photo 1**
- Location: *Unit 3 — North wall*
- Stage: *Framing complete*
- Flag: *None*

**Photo 2**
- Location: *Unit 3 — Interior*
- Stage: *Materials*
- Flag: *Possible damage — sheathing*

**DIALOGUE (Mike, to a passing worker)**
> "Hey, log that damaged sheathing — already on it."

**AUDIO**
- Boots on plywood
- Saw cutting in the distance
- Wind gust

---

## SCENE 4 — AUTO CONSOLIDATION (0:40 – 0:52)

**VISUAL**
Mike sits on the tailgate. He opens the **Today** dashboard. We watch the screen fill in as the day goes — a timeline view.

**ON-SCREEN (dashboard, top to bottom)**
- **Crew on site:** 8
- **Hours logged:** 54.5
- **Tasks completed:** Framing U3, Trenching U2, Rebar tie U1
- **Equipment in use:** Excavator (4h), Nailer, Compressor, Mixer
- **Flags:** 1 — damaged sheathing, Unit 3
- **Photos:** 11

A small banner at the top reads: *"Auto-updated 2 min ago."*

**DIALOGUE (Mike, off-screen)**
> "Everything's already in here."

**AUDIO**
- Background machinery softer now — late afternoon wind-down
- A truck door slams in the distance

---

## SCENE 5 — END-OF-DAY REPORT (0:52 – 1:08)

**VISUAL**
Mike taps **"Generate Daily Report."** A clean PDF preview slides up.

**ON-SCREEN (report preview)**

```
DAILY REPORT — Cedar Lane Project
Date: May 16, 2026
Superintendent: Mike Alvarez

WORK COMPLETED
• Unit 3 framing — complete (Carlos R., Diego M.)
• Unit 2 trenching — 80% (Tom K., Luis F.)
• Unit 1 rebar tie — complete (Jamal P.)

CREW HOURS
8 workers — 54.5 total hours

EQUIPMENT
Excavator (4.0h), Framing nailer, Compressor, Mixer

ISSUES
• Damaged sheathing identified, Unit 3 — replacement needed
• Concrete delivery delayed 40 min

PHOTOS: 11 attached
```

**SPLIT-SCREEN, 4 seconds**

- LEFT: Time-lapse of the old way — Mike at a kitchen table, laptop open, typing, scrolling through WhatsApp, sighing. Clock on wall: **8:42 PM.**
- RIGHT: New way — Mike taps once. Report ready. Clock on dash: **4:31 PM.**

**AUDIO**
- Old side: muted keyboard tapping, dog barking somewhere in the house
- New side: truck idling, the gentle thunk of a tailgate closing

---

## SCENE 6 — FINAL MOMENT (1:08 – 1:18)

**VISUAL**
Mike pulls the truck door shut. Sun lower in the sky. He glances at his phone — the report is already sent. He half-smiles.

**DIALOGUE (Mike, casual, to camera or to himself)**
> "Used to take me 90 minutes… now it's done before I get in the truck."

He starts the engine. Drives off. Hold on the empty site for a beat — framing silhouetted against the late sun.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT (final card)**
*SiteLog — daily reports, done by the time you leave the site.*

**AUDIO**
- Truck engine starts
- Background machinery has stopped — site is winding down
- Light wind
- Fade out

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Camera:** Handheld, slightly shaky. Avoid drone shots or polished cinematography — should feel like it could've been filmed by a foreman with a decent phone.
- **Wardrobe:** Real workwear. Dust on boots. No staged-looking hi-vis.
- **App screen captures:** Use real-looking timestamps and crew names. No Lorem Ipsum. No emojis in the app UI.
- **No music** under scenes 1–4. A very low ambient pad can come in during the split-screen, fade out by the final shot.
- **Avoid:** sci-fi UI flourishes, animated AI sparkles, holograms, voice-of-god narration, the word "revolutionary."
